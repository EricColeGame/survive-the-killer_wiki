## How to Read Any Killer's Stat Card

Before you can counter a killer, you have to know what you are reading. Most killers in Survive the Killer are presented with a small set of stats and one signature ability. Understanding what those stats actually control is the difference between "that killer is overpowered" and "I made a readable mistake."

| Stat / Element | What it controls | Why it matters to you | What to watch for |
|---|---|---|---|
| Movement speed | Base chase speed | Determines whether you can win a straight-line footrace | High speed usually comes with a weaker ability |
| Stamina / sprint | Sprint duration and recovery | Decides how long you can chain loops before slowing down | Low stamina killers want short chases, not long ones |
| Signature ability | Unique power on a cooldown | The real identity of the killer | Learn the audio or visual tell, not the name |
| Ability cooldown | How often the power returns | Tells you when to push objectives and when to hide | Long cooldowns are your window to act |
| Detection / tracking | How the killer finds hidden players | Counters hiding-heavy playstyles | Some killers punish lockers directly |
| Unlock tier | Cost, rarity, or event status | Signals how common the killer is in matchmaking | Rare killers often have unusual, gimmicky kits |

A useful habit: when you die, ask which stat actually killed you. Was it raw speed, the ability, or your own bad hiding choice? That single question turns every loss into a scouting report.

## Frequently Asked Questions

**How many killers are in Survive the Killer?**

The number changes with nearly every major update, and event releases can push it higher temporarily. Rather than memorizing a fixed count, focus on the six archetypes above — that framework covers the Survive the Killer all killers roster no matter how large it grows. The in-game Killers shop and community-maintained wikis are the most reliable places to see the current total.

**Which killer is best for beginners?**

Players generally recommend starting with a straightforward chaser-style killer. Simple kits teach you map layouts, survivor movement habits, and timing without forcing you to manage a complicated ability. Once you can consistently close out chases, move on to an ambusher or trapper to expand your range.

**Can you unlock every killer for free?**

In most versions of the game, yes — a large portion of the roster is reachable through in-game currency earned by playing rounds, with premium currency acting as a shortcut rather than the only path. Expect a grind, and prioritize the killers that match your playstyle instead of buying the whole list.

**Why do I keep getting caught even when I hide well?**

Community reports point to two usual causes: reusing the same hiding spot across rounds, and hiding too early before the killer has committed to a patrol route. Strong survivors rotate both location and timing. If you are still learning the Survive the Killer all killers lineup, treat every hiding spot as a one-time resource and plan your next move before you enter it.

Mastering the roster is really about pattern recognition. Learn the archetypes, respect the cooldowns, vary your hiding, and the same killers that used to end your rounds will start feeling like puzzles you already know how to solve.
