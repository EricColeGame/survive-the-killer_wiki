## Five Survival Principles That Work Against Grimsley

These fundamentals apply to nearly every round of **Survive the Killer**, and they matter even more when a pressure-heavy killer like Grimsley is hunting.

1. **Track the objective bar, not the killer.** Watching the progress meter tells you how much risk is justified. At 20 percent, hide. At 90 percent, push.
2. **Always know two ways out.** Before you touch a single objective, note the nearest escape route and one backup loop. Dead-end objectives are where rounds end badly.
3. **Sprint with purpose.** Sprinting is loud and burns your stamina. Save it for crossing open ground and breaking line of sight, not for casual walking.
4. **Listen before you look.** Audio cues — footsteps, ability sounds, distant interactions — give you a half-second head start that no cosmetic can replace.
5. **Trade position, never tempo.** Getting chased is fine if you are dragging the killer away from teammates who are still working.

| Common mistake | Better habit | Why it matters |
|---|---|---|
| Sprinting everywhere | Walk in cover, sprint in the open | Reduces how easily you are located |
| Hiding in a dead end | Hide near a loop or window | Keeps an escape option available |
| Rushing the last objective alone | Wait for one teammate or a distraction | Denies free downs at the finish line |
| Ignoring audio | Headphones on, volume up | Early warning beats fast reflexes |
| Abandoning downed teammates instantly | Assess the killer's location first | A rescued teammate means more objective speed |

| Phase | Your focus | Error to avoid |

### A Simple Survival Checklist

1. **First 30 seconds:** identify the objective cluster nearest to an escape route.
2. **First minute:** complete one interaction, then reposition.
3. **Mid-round:** keep roughly one room of distance from the killer's last sighting.
4. **Last objectives:** move as a group of two or three and call out rotations.
5. **Exit:** leave when the route is open. Do not loot, do not flex, do not linger.

Community reports consistently show the same pattern: most losses in Survive the Killer happen in the final 20 percent of objective progress, when players get greedy. Grimsley thrives in that window. Your counter is patience — finish the round instead of the highlight reel.
