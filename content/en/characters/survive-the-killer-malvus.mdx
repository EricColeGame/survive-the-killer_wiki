## How to Survive the Killer Malvus Rounds

Surviving a malicious magician is mostly about discipline. Rounds against him break into clear phases, and each one demands a different mindset.

| Round phase | Your priority | Action to take |
|---|---|---|
| Early | Information | Learn the map layout and note where teammates are working |
| Mid | Group pressure | Stay within rescue distance of at least one ally |
| Late | Objective focus | Push progress instead of hiding in corners |
| Endgame | Escape routes | Commit to an exit early — hesitation kills |
| Last survivor | Survival | Play the edges, avoid open ground, and stall smart |

Five habits that separate survivors who escape from survivors who do not:

1. **Never trust a single visual.** If something looks too convenient, it probably is.
2. **Listen more than you look.** Footsteps, ambience shifts, and ability sounds reveal positioning.
3. **Rotate, do not camp.** Sitting in one hiding spot gives Malvus time to close the map around you.
4. **Keep your team in view.** Isolation is how most survivors get eliminated against mind-game killers.
5. **Plan your exit before you need it.** Knowing two routes out of any zone beats reacting in the moment.

If you only remember one rule from this survive the killer malvus breakdown, make it this: hesitating at a decision point is far more dangerous than making a confident, imperfect move.

Practical killer tips from community reports:

- **Use tells deliberately.** A visible wind-up that fakes one direction can pull survivors into a worse position.
- **Target the isolated player.** Coordination is the survivor's best tool; removing one link weakens the chain.
- **Vary your tempo.** Alternating pressure and patience makes you much harder to read.
- **Play the objectives, not the chase.** The round ends on objectives, not on one dramatic elimination attempt.

Playing Malvus well is a long-term project. New Malvus players tend to over-rely on his theme and under-rely on fundamentals like route control and timing.
